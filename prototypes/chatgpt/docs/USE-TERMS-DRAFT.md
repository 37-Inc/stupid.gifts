# Using Stupid Gifts in ChatGPT

Stupid Gifts is operated by [[Confirm the legal publisher]]. These terms concern its gift-discovery app in ChatGPT. Use the app only if you are eligible to use ChatGPT under its applicable terms.

The app helps you discover unusual physical gifts from the Stupid Gifts catalog using interests you describe. It can show product photos, brief descriptions and links to corresponding Stupid Gifts pages. You can refine interests and exclusions through the conversation. Results depend on the eligible catalog, so the app may have few or no suitable suggestions.

The app does not provide current prices, budget filtering, guaranteed stock or delivery dates. It does not place orders, take payment, reserve products, deliver gifts, manage retailer accounts or track purchases. Check the product and retailer pages before deciding whether to buy, including the actual variant, suitability, warnings, price, availability and delivery terms. A recommendation is a discovery aid, not a promise that a product suits every recipient.

If you choose a retailer link on the Stupid Gifts website, the retailer handles its own purchase and payment process, delivery, returns and customer-service arrangements. Stupid Gifts may earn affiliate commissions from qualifying purchases. The app itself does not complete that transaction or change the retailer's terms.

Please submit only the information needed to describe gift interests. Do not send passwords, payment information or sensitive recipient details through the app, and do not use it to attempt unsupported transactions or bypass service protections. The app may return an error or no results when information is unavailable or a request is outside its supported scope.

The app and catalog can change or be temporarily unavailable. We may correct product information, remove unavailable or ineligible items, and update the service. Questions about the app can be sent to [goosegifts@37.technology](mailto:goosegifts@37.technology); purchase and order questions belong with the retailer. The app privacy notice describes how Stupid Gifts handles information, separately from ChatGPT and retailer practices.
