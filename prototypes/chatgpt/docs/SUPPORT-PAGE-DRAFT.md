# Help with Stupid Gifts in ChatGPT

Stupid Gifts helps you find unusual physical gifts by interest and browse their product pages. For help with app results, broken photos or links, accessibility, or a privacy question, email [goosegifts@37.technology](mailto:goosegifts@37.technology).

Tell us briefly what happened, approximately when it happened, and which device or ChatGPT app you were using. If relevant, include the Stupid Gifts product-page link. Share only what is needed to explain the issue. Do not send payment-card details, passwords, verification codes, sensitive information about a gift recipient, or an entire private chat transcript.

The app does not display current prices, guarantee availability or delivery, place orders, or access retailer accounts. For payment, shipment, cancellation, return or refund questions, contact the retailer through the retailer's own support channels.

You can refine gift interests or exclusions in the conversation. If the service reports no suitable matches or a temporary error, try a more specific interest or return later; do not assume an unavailable gift has been ordered or reserved.
